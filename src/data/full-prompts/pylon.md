Create a static HTML+CSS+JS website for "PYLON AI — Every Model. Your Infrastructure.", a self-hosted AI platform landing page. Features: fixed bookmark ribbon + transparent→solid nav, fullscreen hero with autoplaying background video + left-aligned white text, dual-row infinite marquee ticker, horizontal feature list with bordered rows, teal-tinted Ecosystem card grid (4 cards with glassmorphism floating mockups), two-column Research split (video left + text right), 500vh sticky scroll-driven Collage section with typing text + parallax portraits, Stats counter grid, cinematic footer with video bleed + gradient overlay.
