# Streetwear Campaign Recreation Prompt

## Objective

Recreate the reference fashion campaign with an almost identical
composition, mood, styling, and urban atmosphere while remaining an
original image.

## Prompt

``` text
Ultra-realistic luxury streetwear editorial photographed in the middle of a wide modern Asian boulevard under a clear blue sky.

Aspect ratio: 16:9.

Three young fashion models wearing oversized monochrome streetwear. Center model faces camera confidently, left model looks sideways, right model faces away. Wide fisheye perspective (~14mm), camera positioned low near street level. Massive empty intersection, contemporary glass buildings, trees lining the sidewalks, bright midday sunlight, crisp shadows, clean asphalt, cinematic urban fashion campaign.

Oversized faded black and grey t-shirts, extra-wide baggy denim cargo jeans, chunky sneakers, silver accessories, messy textured hairstyles. Editorial styling inspired by contemporary Korean/Japanese street fashion.

Luxury commercial photography, ultra sharp, HDR, natural skin tones, subtle film look, premium apparel campaign, magazine quality, 8K.

No crowds. No vehicles near the models. No text overlays. No logos except subtle garment branding. Symmetrical composition.
```

## Match Instructions

``` text
Match:
- camera height
- fisheye distortion
- subject spacing
- pose
- city perspective
- sky color
- lighting direction
- street width
- building proportions
- clothing silhouette
- overall framing

Avoid copying trademarks or identifiable branding.
```
