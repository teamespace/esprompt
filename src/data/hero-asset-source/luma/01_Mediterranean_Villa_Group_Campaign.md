# Mediterranean Villa Group Campaign Prompt

## Objective

Recreate a luxury European resort fashion campaign with nearly identical
composition, lighting, wardrobe palette, and architectural atmosphere.

## Prompt

``` text
Ultra-realistic luxury resort fashion editorial photographed during golden hour outside an elegant Mediterranean stone villa.

Aspect ratio: 16:9.

Three fashion models stand naturally beside a historic limestone balustrade. Warm cream cable-knit sweaters, polos and cardigans with tailored white trousers. Neutral loafers and minimalist sunglasses. Models look in different directions with relaxed sophisticated expressions.

Background includes Tuscan hills, cypress trees, stone villa with dark green shutters, terracotta planters filled with white flowers and warm honey-colored sunlight.

Luxury quiet lifestyle, old-money aesthetic, premium knitwear campaign, cinematic photography, 85mm full-frame lens, shallow depth of field, soft golden light, realistic skin tones, subtle film grading, 8K, magazine quality.

No crowds. No text. No modern distractions.
```

## Match Instructions

``` text
Match framing, balustrade placement, villa architecture, cypress trees, model spacing, warm color grading, wardrobe palette, sunlight direction and negative space while avoiding exact branded logos.
```
