# Luxury Tennis Lifestyle Prompt

## Objective

Recreate an elegant tennis-inspired lifestyle campaign with premium
resort aesthetics.

## Prompt

``` text
Ultra-realistic luxury fashion editorial beside a clay tennis court at golden hour.

Aspect ratio: 16:9.

Young woman with long dark hair wearing oversized sunglasses and a cream cable-knit V-neck tennis sweater with navy trim. She sits casually on a dark green wooden bench. A luxury canvas duffel bag rests beside her. Folded knitwear and a vintage wooden tennis racket lie on the bench. Clay court, tennis net, manicured hedges, tall cypress trees and distant Mediterranean hills behind her.

Warm cinematic sunlight, premium lifestyle photography, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, elegant old-money aesthetic, soft film color grading, photorealistic, 8K.

No text. No crowds.
```

## Match Instructions

``` text
Match pose, bench angle, tennis court layout, wardrobe colors, golden lighting, background trees, prop placement and framing without reproducing trademarks.
```
