# Mediterranean Walk Campaign Prompt

## Objective

Recreate the refined resort fashion campaign with similar composition
and atmosphere.

## Prompt

``` text
Ultra-realistic luxury fashion campaign photographed outside a Mediterranean estate during golden hour.

Aspect ratio: 16:9.

A stylish couple walks naturally along a gravel driveway beside an elegant stone villa. Woman wears a cream cable-knit cardigan with burgundy trim and tailored white trousers. Man wears a cream knit polo, white tailored trousers, loafers, sunglasses and carries a brown leather weekend bag.

Foreground features textured gravel. Background includes manicured hedges, cypress trees, terracotta planters, a classic red vintage convertible sports car and golf bags. Warm evening sunlight creates long soft shadows. Cinematic luxury resort mood, old-money aesthetic, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, 8K.

No text. Minimal composition.
```

## Match Instructions

``` text
Match walking pose, vehicle placement, villa perspective, landscaping, wardrobe palette, lighting direction, camera distance and cinematic grading while avoiding exact brand marks.
```
