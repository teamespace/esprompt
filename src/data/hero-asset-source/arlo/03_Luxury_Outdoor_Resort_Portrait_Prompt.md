# Luxury Outdoor Resort Portrait Prompt

## Objective

Recreate the elegant outdoor resort fashion portrait with similar
composition and atmosphere.

## Prompt

``` text
Ultra-realistic luxury resort menswear campaign photographed outdoors during golden hour.

Aspect ratio: 16:9.

A young man sits casually on a long minimalist concrete bench integrated into modern architecture. He wears an off-white knit long-sleeve polo, cream tailored trousers, and brown suede loafers. One arm rests on the bench while he gazes into the distance.

Minimal architecture with pale stone flooring, soft neutral concrete, expansive sky occupying most of the frame, faint clouds, distant rocky landscape on the far left. Warm natural sunlight with soft shadows. Eye-level composition, cinematic luxury fashion campaign, premium lifestyle photography, 8K, photorealistic.

Calm Mediterranean aesthetic. No text. No additional props.
```

## Match Instructions

``` text
Match bench proportions, camera height, sky-to-subject ratio, pose, warm color grading, clothing palette, architectural minimalism, and overall visual balance. Avoid reproducing branded elements.
```
